Privacy Policy for First Aid Pop! - 急救先鋒

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

First Aid Pop ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding your data when you use our mobile application.

2. Data Collection and Usage

First Aid Pop does not collect, transmit, distribute, or sell any personal information or identifiable user data. All gameplay, rescue progress, and local settings are processed entirely on your device.

3. Leaderboard and Online Features

If you choose to submit a game score to the public leaderboard, only your submitted custom nickname and score are stored on our database. No device identifiers, location, or private personal data are collected.

4. Third-Party Services

The app does not incorporate third-party advertising SDKs or tracking tools that monitor your activity across other apps or websites.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@lohascare.com
